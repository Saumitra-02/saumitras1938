# Starts Are Important

The way I start can make the majority of the difference to how the rest of my day goes.

And this isn't just about what I do immediately after waking up. It also applies to how I restart work after any kind of break, whether it's lunch, a walk, or a bio-break.

I know I shouldn't be listening to songs or podcasts while working. However, when I try to restart work after a break, I often find myself craving songs or podcasts while working.

If I resist this temptation and push through the discomfort of getting started, I can work properly until the next break or the completion of the session.

But if I give in to the temptation, I may end up wanting to listen to a more interesting song or podcast, then browse other things, and soon find myself going down a distraction rabbit hole. At that point, starting meaningful work again feels quite difficult.

The only way out of such situations seems to be going for a walk or doing some more vigorous exercise.
