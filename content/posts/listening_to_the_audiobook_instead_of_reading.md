# Listening to the Audiobook Instead of Reading

I woke up at 6:30 AM and started reading *The Master...*, a book about Roger Federer.

My intention was to read until my sleepiness went away and then get started with my morning chores. However, I ended up reading until around 9:45 AM, when the rest of the family started their morning chores.

So, was this a failure?

Perhaps waking up at 6:30 AM and staying up was a success. However, I should probably have stopped reading and started my morning chores much earlier.

## What to Try Next

To address this, I've decided to try listening to the audiobook version instead of reading.

I'll start listening to the audiobook and simultaneously doing my morning chores, after solely reading for 15 minutes.

This way, I can still enjoy the book without letting reading take over my morning.
