## The Problem

During work sessions, I've found it very difficult to get started and, after a while, to keep going. Many times, I get distracted even before starting and end up wasting quite a bit of time.

In the office, I'm able to start working naturally. This is probably due to a combination of factors:

- Deadline pressure.
- The commute to the office.
- The presence of other people working around me.
- Bright lighting and a more stimulating environment.

However, at the library, I struggle both to start working and to sustain my focus.

Even taking regular walks hasn't improved the situation significantly.

## What I've Tried

### Hiking

I tried going for a hike on a nearby hill, and that seemed to work better. However, its effect didn't last very long either.

A regular walk didn't have the same effect.

### Running

I also tried running, which seemed to work quite well.

I would run along Gandhi Bhavan Road and back every 1.5 hours. This gave me good results.

The problem, though, is that people from my society or the library might see me running.

## Trying a Gym Membership

I'm thinking of getting a gym membership and trying it out for a month.

Instead of running along Gandhi Bhavan Road, I could do some light workouts at the gym every 1–2 hours during work sessions. Bit more private.

I could also use it for morning workouts on weekends, and on weekdays, if I have the time. This may also help me get productive more quickly on weekends, as the morning workout is generally the hurdle to clear before I feel like starting my morning chores. This may also force me to bathe quicker on weekend mornings, after I return home from the gym.

Let's see how that goes!

## Gyms to Consider

- Avenue – if it turns out to be a gym I like, this would be the most convenient option.
- Wildfit – near Swami Samarth Temple.
- Sachin Tendulkar Gym – near Paranjape School.

I'll probably need to take my bike to the library regularly, unless Avenue turns out to be a suitable gym. The other two options are a bit far away.

## What I Want to Find Out

Can short gym sessions every 1–2 hours help me start work more easily, regain focus when I get distracted, and sustain productive work sessions for longer?

I'll try it for a month and see how it goes.
