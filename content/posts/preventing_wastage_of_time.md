## Possible Solutions

### 1. Avoid Going Home for Lunch

I shouldn't have come home from Starbucks for lunch.

Next time:

- Leave home on Saturday morning.
- Have lunch and dinner outside, or prepare and pack food before leaving for the library.
- Follow the same plan on Sunday if the work isn't completed on Saturday.

The goal is to avoid returning home and getting distracted during the middle of a workday.

### 2. Take Another Walk When Stuck

On Saturday evening, after wasting an hour even after the hike, I should have gone for another walk instead of sitting around, feeling bad, and continuing to waste time.

**Action:** When stuck in a distraction loop, change my environment or go for a walk instead of waiting to feel motivated.

### 3. Go to Sleep When the Urge to Waste Time Appears at Night

At night, when I start feeling like wasting time, I should go to sleep instead.

### 4. Make It Easier to Resume Work

If I leave my laptop asleep overnight:

- Keep the work screen open.
- Leave simple instructions for how to restart work.
- Avoid leaving a distracting screen open.

The idea is to reduce the friction involved in getting started the next day.
