# Temptation Bundling for stepping out of the house

Today, lunch ended around 6 PM. I had planned to go to the library afterwards, but instead, I sat on the sofa and started reading a book.

I thought I would leave once I had finished reading a little. However, I ended up falling asleep, even after switching to a different book after a while.

This has happened a few times before, so I tried thinking of some possible solutions.

## Possible Solutions

One idea was to leave the house early in the morning, when I have more energy, and either pack lunch or have it outside. However, this isn't always possible. For example, lunch might not be ready, or my family might not allow me to leave before eating.

## Trying Temptation Bundling

I'm thinking of introducing an incentive for going to the library.

The idea is simple: **if I go to the library and put my bag on a chair, I'm allowed to have a pastry or some other food item that I particularly enjoy.**

This is an example of temptation bundling: pairing something I want to do with something I enjoy.

Hopefully, this will make stepping out of the house and getting to the library more appealing, even when I'm feeling reluctant to leave.
